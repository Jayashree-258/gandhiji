Mahathma Gandhi

Early Life and Education

• Born as Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi on October 2, 1869, in Porbandar, India.
• Married Kasturba at age 13 in an arranged marriage.
• Studied law in London, England, from 1888 to 1891.

Work in South Africa

• Moved to South Africa in 1893 to work for an Indian firm.
• Faced racial discrimination and fought for the civil rights of Indians.
• Created Satyagraha, a method of nonviolent protest and devotion to truth.

Indian Independence Movement

• Returned to India in 1915 and joined the nationalist movement.
• Led major peaceful campaigns, including the Non-Cooperation Movement and the Quit India Movement.
• Walked 240 miles in the famous 1930 Salt March to protest British salt taxes.
• Earned the title Mahatma, which means "Great Soul".

Legacy and Death

• India gained independence from British rule in 1947.
• Assassinated on January 30, 1948, in Delhi by Nathuram Godse.
• October 2 is celebrated globally as the International Day of Non-Violence and nationally as Gandhi Jayanti.
